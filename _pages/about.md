Hi! I am a second-year PhD student in the [Department of Management Science and Engineering](https://msande.stanford.edu/) at Stanford University, where I am fortunate to be advised by Professors [Jose Blanchet](https://msande.stanford.edu/people/jose-blanchet) and [Renyuan Xu](https://renyuanxu.github.io/).

Prior to Stanford, I obtained my MMath in Mathematics and Statistics (integrated bachelor's and master's) from [Lincoln College](https://lincoln.ox.ac.uk/), University of Oxford. I am very grateful to have worked with [Prof. Seth Flaxman](https://sethrf.com/) during the summer before my third year and throughout that year. I completed my Dissertation under the supervision of [Prof. George Deligiannidis](https://ziorzis.github.io/).

I am interested in how tools from statistics and stochastic control can help understand modern machine learning, particularly generative models. My current work focuses on continuous-time reinforcement learning and the theory of diffusion models.

Please feel free to reach out if you would like to chat.
