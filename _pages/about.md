Hi! I am a first-year PhD student in the [Department of Management Science and Engineering](https://msande.stanford.edu/) at Stanford University.

Prior to Stanford, I obtained my MMath in Mathematics and Statistics (integrated bachelor's and master's) from [Lincoln College](https://lincoln.ox.ac.uk/), University of Oxford. I am very grateful to have worked with [Prof. Seth Flaxman](https://sethrf.com/) during the summer before my third year and throughout that year. I completed my Dissertation under the supervision of [Prof. George Deligiannidis](https://ziorzis.github.io/).

I am broadly interested in applications of optimal transport methods, the theory of diffusion models, stochastic control, and continuous-time reinforcement learning methods.

Please feel free to reach out if you would like to chat.
